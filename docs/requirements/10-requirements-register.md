| REQ-036 | **Outsourced goods & procurement (buy-in → repack → resell).** The product catalog (Item) is the single "ultimate product list" showing product-wise stock status. Items carry a **vendor link (M:N to vendor Contacts)** as a standing supplier list. A lightweight **Procurement event** brings outsourced stock in: +StockMovement (reason=`purchase`) on an outsourced batch **and** a vendor **expense** Transaction. Enables the in-house vs outsourced split in all reports and resale-margin per bought-in batch. **Negative stock is allowed** (see note) as a procurement signal. | Inventory / Procurement | v1             | confirmed | /arch-design clarification 2026-10-14                |

## Open modeling decisions (deferred to Phase 3 — Architecture, NOT decided now)

- **OD-1:** Whether categorized entities are one table with a `type` field or separate tables — pure design choice; requirement is only that categories exist (REQ-001) and money flows both ways (REQ-002).
- **OD-2 (RESOLVED 2026-10-14):** Employees are modelled as **entities** (they carry salary/labour expense); customers/visitors/vendors are **contacts**; owners/family are **app users**. See REQ-001, REQ-014, REQ-021, REQ-031.
  - ⚠ **Supersedes** the earlier big-picture statement (Capture 1 and `00-big-picture.md` line 38) that *"contacts should have the farm owner's role and the employee's roles"*. The big-picture file is kept verbatim; this note records the override (review F2).
- **OD-3 (deferred):** Inventory **deduction timing** — at which order status stock is reserved/drawn down. User: "will see later." Revisit in Phase 3 / detailed design (ties to REQ-011b in v2).
- **OD-4 (closed):** Hardware / IoT (scales, sensors) — **none in scope** (REQ-029).
- **OD-5 (deferred — added 2026-10-14):** **Batch P&L cost-allocation method** — how raw-material cost is split across branched outputs (husk/shell/pulp) and how shared overhead (e.g. electricity) is allocated into per-batch P&L. v1 = **direct-tag only** (REQ-010); allocation modelling deferred to Phase 3 (review F8).
